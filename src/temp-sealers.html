Surface Protection

Why Microcement and Decorative Concrete Must Be Sealed

Microcement is a high-performance mineral finish. Like all cementitious surfaces, it is porous by nature. Without a protective sealer, microcement will absorb water, stains and contaminants — significantly reducing surface life and appearance.

A professional sealer transforms a decorative mineral surface into a durable, high-performance finish suitable for residential floors, wet areas and commercial environments.

INCISE sealers are formulated specifically for decorative mineral systems, preserving surface aesthetics while delivering long-term protection.

◈

Abrasion Resistance

Protects against foot traffic wear and surface degradation.

◈

Water Protection

Prevents moisture penetration in wet areas and outdoor surfaces.

◈

Stain Resistance

Creates a barrier against oils, food and chemical stains.

◈

UV Stability

Maintains clarity and colour without yellowing under sunlight.

◈

Easy Maintenance

Sealed surfaces are simple to clean and maintain long-term.

◈

Aesthetic Preservation

Enhances and locks in the natural beauty of mineral finishes.

Technical Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from architects, contractors and clients specifying microcement and decorative concrete sealer systems.

What is the best sealer for microcement floors?

Polyurethane sealers such as INCISE PU WB Sealer provide excellent abrasion resistance and water protection, making them the preferred specification for residential microcement floors in kitchens, bathrooms and living areas. For higher-traffic applications, INCISE RapidShield offers superior mechanical resistance.

Can microcement be sealed with water-based sealers?

Yes. Water-based sealers are widely used for microcement, particularly for interior spaces where low VOC emissions and natural matte finishes are specified. Both INCISE WB Sealer and INCISE PU WB Sealer are water-based systems suitable for microcement surfaces.

What is the difference between polyurethane and acrylic sealers?

Polyurethane sealers deliver higher abrasion resistance and long-term durability, making them ideal for residential and light commercial floors under regular foot traffic. Acrylic sealers such as INCISE 2K HR enhance colour depth and provide a durable protective film suitable for both interior and exterior decorative concrete applications.

What is a polyaspartic sealer?

Polyaspartic coatings are advanced two-component protective sealers known for extremely fast curing times and high mechanical and chemical resistance. INCISE RapidShield is an aliphatic polyaspartic system delivering industrial-grade protection, making it ideal for commercial floors, restaurants, retail spaces and garages where rapid return to service is required.

Do microcement floors need to be sealed?

Yes. Microcement is a porous mineral finish that requires sealing to achieve water resistance, stain resistance and long-term durability. Unsealed microcement will absorb moisture and contaminants, leading to staining and surface deterioration. Sealing is not optional — it is an integral part of every INCISE microcement system.

Which sealer is best for commercial floors in Uganda and Kenya?

For commercial and high-traffic floors in East Africa, INCISE RapidShield polyaspartic sealer is the recommended specification. It provides industrial-grade protection with ultra-fast curing, UV stability and chemical resistance — proven performance for retail spaces, hospitality floors and showrooms across Uganda and Kenya.
